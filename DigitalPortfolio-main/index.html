About me

Bringing concepts to life, one layer at a time.

I'm Wafiqa Hoosain, an up-and-coming graphic designer based in Cape Town and currently in my second year at the Cape Peninsula University of Technology (CPUT). Flutter Studio is the home for my work.

My work moves between the sketchbook and the screen, from vector brand marks and editorial layouts to ink drawings, mixed-media letterforms and photo-composited posters. I'm drawn to depth, contrast and organic shapes, and to the moment a loose, hand-drawn idea becomes a sharp, considered piece of design.

Every brief is a chance to experiment. I start by hand, push the idea until it feels unexpected, then refine it digitally until every line earns its place.
